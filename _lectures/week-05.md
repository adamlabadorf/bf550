## Topic

Evaluation & generalization: how to tell whether a classifier actually works. We cover
precision/recall, ROC/AUC, and confusion matrices, then the core ideas that make evaluation
honest — overfitting and underfitting, the bias–variance tradeoff, train/validation/test
splits, cross-validation, and how data leakage quietly inflates results. We return to the
Week 4 rRNA classifier and evaluate it properly.
